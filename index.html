Research and writing

Papers and case studies from my graduate work, 2026.

  1. From Keywords to Context: Domain-Specific Retrieval-Augmented Generation as the Next Evolution of Enterprise Knowledge Management

    Research paper · Knowledge Management and Business Intelligence · Seattle Pacific University

    Argues that domain-specific retrieval-augmented generation systems are the next evolution of enterprise knowledge management infrastructure. It draws on my experience building one for a bank.

    Read the paper (PDF)

  2. Detecting Conflict-of-Interest Exposure in Industry Payments to Clinicians: A Batch, Streaming, and Machine Learning Pipeline for Mephisto Health System

    Research paper · Big Data and Cloud Platforms · Seattle Pacific University

    Designs and evaluates a pipeline on the public CMS Open Payments dataset: 16.1 million payment records covering about one million clinicians. Batch screening, streaming alerts and K-means clustering narrow that population to a shortlist of 500 for a compliance team to review. Mephisto is a fictional health system, and the dashboard says what the scores are for: review prioritization, not proof of wrongdoing.

    Read the paper (PDF)

    Case study · Information Systems Security · Seattle Pacific University

  4. SIM Swap Fraud on M-Pesa

  5. Patient Pharmacovigilance Text Mining

    Data mining project · Seattle Pacific University · Feb 2026

Industry · Absa Bank Kenya · 2024 to 2025

A retrieval-augmented product assistant for bank staff

Staff needed quick, accurate answers from a long product catalogue. I designed and built a document retrieval system that searches the catalogue by meaning and answers in plain language, with the response streamed as it is generated.

I built the document pipeline (chunking and sentence embeddings), the FAISS vector index, the FastAPI service and the LLM integration, deployed it on AWS EC2 against an internal sandbox, and documented the API for the teams who came after me. This is the system my paper on enterprise knowledge management grows out of.

  • FastAPI
  • LangChain
  • FAISS
  • Hugging Face embeddings
  • Mistral-7B-Instruct
  • AWS EC2

Aug 2026 · Graduate project

Credit risk classification

Classification model in Python and scikit-learn with a three-tab Tableau dashboard: a risk profile of past borrowers, a queue that sorts applications into approve, review and disapprove, and a live what-if calculator built on logistic regression. The calculator states its own accuracy (65% on test data, ROC-AUC 0.74) and that it is for exploration, not final decisions.

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Tableau

Feb 2026 · Graduate project

Antimicrobial resistance prediction

KNIME pipeline that predicts ciprofloxacin resistance in urinary tract infections, so the right antibiotic can be chosen before lab results come back. Built on Stanford Healthcare's public, de-identified resistance dataset (283,715 patients; 10,494 UTI cases). Gradient boosted trees reached an AUC of 0.784.

  • KNIME
  • Gradient boosted trees
  • SMOTE
